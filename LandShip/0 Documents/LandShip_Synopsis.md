# VehicleTrax: Your Complete Vehicle Management Solution

## Take Control of Your Fleet with Confidence

**VehicleTrax** is the comprehensive vehicle tracking and maintenance management application designed for vehicle owners who demand precision, organization, and insight. Whether you manage a single car, an RV, a work truck, or an entire fleet, LandShip transforms scattered receipts and mental checklists into a powerful, actionable database that follows you everywhere—synced seamlessly across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac via iCloud.

## Why VehicleTrax?

### Every Mile, Every Dollar, Every Detail—In One Place

Stop juggling spreadsheets, shoeboxes of receipts, and forgotten service intervals. VehicleTrax captures everything that matters:

- **Garage Management**: Catalog every vehicle (auto, motorhome, travel trailer, utility trailer...) you own or operate with complete specifications, photos, documentation, VINs, insurance details, and tire pressures.

- **Fuel Tracking**: Log every fill-up with automatic fuel economy calculations (MPG, L/100km, km/L), cost-per-mile analysis, price trends, and receipt photos. See exactly how each vehicle performs over time and spot expensive patterns before they become problems.

- **Trip Logging**: Record business and personal trips with start/end odometer readings, timestamps, purposes, and routes.  If you are towing a vehicle (travel trailer, utility trailer, toad...), tick the 'Towed Vehicle' 'option and select the vehicle from the database on the travel log.  Milage for this travel log will be updated to the towed vehicles database record and saved as 'virtual milage' to differentiate from actual milage. 

- **Service History**: Build a complete maintenance record with vendor details, parts used, labor costs, and work-performed notes. Never miss another oil change or tire rotation—VehicleTrax tracks intervals and reminds you when service is due.

- **Parts Catalog**: Define consumables and components once (filters, fluids, brake pads) then reuse them across service templates and records. Track costs, preferred vendors, SKUs, and compatibility notes for fast reordering and consistent pricing.

- **Maintenance Planning**: Create reusable Service Item templates (e.g., "Oil & Filter Change") with default parts, labor estimates, and intervals. When work is performed, one-tap entry from the template speeds data capture and ensures consistency.

- **Systems & Vendors**: Centralize your controlled vocabularies—vehicle systems (Engine, Brakes, Cooling), service categories, and vendor/shop directories—so pickers are clean, reports are accurate, and your data stays organized.

### Designed for Real-World Workflows

- **Multi-Platform Native Experience**: Built with SwiftUI and SwiftData for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. Enjoy a fast, responsive interface that feels natural on, and adapts to, every Apple device you own.

- **iCloud Sync**: Your data is stored locally for speed and privacy, then synced seamlessly via iCloud. Add a fuel log on your iPhone at the pump, review service history on your iPad at the shop, and generate reports from your Mac—all automatically in sync.

- **Flexible Units & Formatting**: Configure once, display everywhere. Choose your preferred distance (mi/km), volume (gal/L), fuel economy (MPG, L/100km), currency, and date formats. Vehicle-specific units drive calculations while global settings control reports and exports.

- **Rich Attachments**: Capture photos of receipts, invoices, vehicle damage, work performed, and installation guides. Store up to five images per fuel log, trip, service record, part, or vehicle—synced and searchable across devices.

- **Professional Reporting**: Generate PDF exports for any vehicle or your entire fleet. Group by date range, vendor, system, or category.

### Intelligence Where It Counts

VehicleTrax doesn't just store data—it makes sense of it:

- **Fuel Economy Trends**: See consumption and cost-per-mile statistics since your last fill-up and lifetime averages per vehicle.

- **Total Cost of Ownership**: Aggregate fuel, parts, labor, and service costs to understand what each vehicle truly costs to operate.

- **Service Intervals & Reminders**: Track maintenance by mileage, engine hours, or calendar intervals. Spot overdue items before breakdowns occur.

- **Vendor Analytics**: Compare shops and suppliers by cost, reliability, and specialization to make smarter service decisions.

- **Virtual Odometer**: For RVs and towed vehicles, VehicleTrax estimates true mileage based on trip data, accounting for towing and non-driven periods.

### Privacy and Control You Can Trust

- **Your Data, Your Device**: Everything is stored locally on your devices and synced privately via your iCloud account—no third-party servers, no tracking, no ads.

- **Backup & Restore**: Create point-in-time snapshots of your database (with or without attachments) and save them to Files, iCloud Drive, or external storage. Restore with confidence when upgrading devices or recovering from mistakes.

- **Active/Inactive Management**: Mark vehicles, parts, or records inactive to declutter lists without losing history. Reactivate anytime or filter by status to include legacy data in reports.

## Who Benefits from VehicleTrax?

- **Small Business Operators**: Track mileage and expenses for multiple vehicles to maximize deductions and manage operating costs.
- **RV & Fleet Enthusiasts**: Monitor maintenance schedules, fuel economy, and service history across cars, trucks, trailers, and motorhomes.

## Get Started in Minutes

1. **Add Your Vehicles**: Enter make, model, year, VIN, and initial odometer (60 available parameters).
2. **Set Your Preferences**: Choose units, currency, and default options once—they sync everywhere.
3. **Log as You Go**: Capture fuel fill-ups, trips, and service as they happen with the iPhone application.
4. **Plan & Schedule**: Define Service Items for recurring maintenance and link parts from your catalog.
5. **Review & Report**: Generate insights, export for paper records stored in the vehicle, and make data-driven decisions about your fleet.
