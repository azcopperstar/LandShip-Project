NOTES
- NauticalTrax is a personal vessel recordkeeping tool. It does not replace a licensed marine surveyor's report or your vessel's official maintenance and safety records — always consult a qualified surveyor for insurance, safety, or pre-purchase decisions.
- Email us (using the above link or the link on the Resources tab) with suggestions for improvements, application features, or issues with the application.

ADDED

## Vessels
- Marine terminology throughout: Marina, Engine Hours, HIN, and Voyage Log replace their land-vehicle equivalents everywhere in the app.
- Haul-Out Records and Survey Records: dedicated records for tracking bottom jobs, zincs, and surveyor reports.
- Mariner Credentials and Sea Service: track licenses/certifications and sea service entries.

## Fuel Log
- Marine Fuel Types: the Fuel Type picker now shows marine-correct fuels (Marine Gasoline, LSMGO, VLSFO, LNG, Methanol, and more) grouped by category, instead of the land-vehicle Gasoline/Diesel/EV/Hybrid list.
- Configurable fuel list: choose which fuel types appear in the picker under Settings > Fuel Types — the selection syncs across your devices.
