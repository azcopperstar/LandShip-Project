Version: 2026.09.07 Build: 108
ADDED

## Fluid Checks
- Marine-specific fluid check items: the Fluid Checks popup (Fuel Log and Travel Log) now shows vessel items — Engine Oil, Coolant, Transmission/Gear Oil, Power Steering Fluid, Hydraulic Steering Fluid, Fuel/Water Separator, Bilge Pump Check, and Stuffing Box/Shaft Seal — instead of the land-vehicle list.
- Configurable in Settings: choose which items appear under Settings > Fluid Checks.

## Location
- Nearest marina lookup: the Location field on Fuel Log and Travel Log lists the 2 closest marinas by name and distance, each with a "Use" button.
- Current location shown: a caption below the field displays your current latitude/longitude.
- Home Location: set a Home location in Settings from your current position — it becomes the top "Use" choice whenever it's closer than the nearest marina.

ADDED

## Vessels
- Marine terminology throughout: Marina, Engine Hours, HIN, and Voyage Log replace their land-vehicle equivalents everywhere in the app.
- Haul-Out Records and Survey Records: dedicated records for tracking bottom jobs, zincs, and surveyor reports.
- Mariner Credentials and Sea Service: track licenses/certifications and sea service entries.

## Fuel Log
- Marine Fuel Types: the Fuel Type picker now shows marine-correct fuels (Marine Gasoline, LSMGO, VLSFO, LNG, Methanol, and more) grouped by category, instead of the land-vehicle Gasoline/Diesel/EV/Hybrid list.
- Configurable fuel list: choose which fuel types appear in the picker under Settings > Fuel Types — the selection syncs across your devices.
